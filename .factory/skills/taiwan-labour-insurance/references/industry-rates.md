# Occupational Accident Insurance Industry Rates (職業災害保險行業別費率)

Effective: January 1, 2025 (reviewed every 3 years)
Source: Bureau of Labor Insurance / Ministry of Labor
URL: https://www.bli.gov.tw/0103189.html

## Rate Structure

Total OAI Rate = Industry Rate (行業別災害費率) + Commuting Rate (上下班災害費率)

- **Commuting rate**: flat **0.07%** for all industries
- **Industry rate**: **0.05% to 0.89%** depending on industry
- **Average combined**: **0.21%** (industry avg 0.14% + commuting 0.07%)

## Experience Rating (實績費率)

For companies with **70+ employees**:
- BLI reviews actual claims over the past 3 years
- Industry rate can be adjusted **up or down by up to 50%**
- Adjusted Rate = Base Industry Rate x (1 + Experience Modifier)
- Modifier range: -0.50 to +0.50
