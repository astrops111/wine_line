# Typhoon Day (颱風天/颱風假) Pay Rules

Source: Ministry of Labor (勞動部)
Legal basis: 天然災害發生事業單位勞工出勤管理及工資給付要點 (amended 2025-09-19)
URL: https://laws.mol.gov.tw/FLAW/FLAWDAT0202.aspx?id=FL049533

---

## Critical Point: A Typhoon Day is NOT a Legal Holiday

**「颱風假不是假」** — The MOL explicitly states a typhoon day is not a holiday.

It is a **force majeure work suspension** (不可抗力之停止工作), not:
- A national holiday (國定假日)
- A regular day off (例假)
- A rest day (休息日)
- Any form of statutory leave (法定假別)

The governing guidelines use **「宜」(should/recommended)**, not **「應」(must/shall)**,
for most pay provisions — meaning they are administrative recommendations, not
enforceable mandates with penalties.

---

## Three-Location Principle (三地任一)

A worker may lawfully not attend if **ANY ONE** of these areas has an announced closure:

1. **工作地** — the city/county where the workplace is located
2. **居住地** — the city/county where the worker lives
3. **上班必經途中** — any city/county on the normal commute route

The employer cannot require proof of commute route.

## Pay Rules by Scenario

### Monthly-Salary Workers (月薪制)

#### Does NOT work on typhoon day (regular workday)

| Rule | Mandatory? |
|:---|:---:|
| Employer SHOULD NOT deduct wages (宜不扣發工資) | Recommended (宜) |
| Employer MUST NOT treat as unauthorized absence (曠工) | **Mandatory** |
| Employer MUST NOT force use of annual/personal leave | **Mandatory** |
| Employer MUST NOT deduct attendance bonus (全勤獎金) | **Mandatory** |
| Employer MUST NOT require make-up work (補行工作) | **Mandatory** |

In practice, most employers pay full monthly salary without deduction.

#### DOES work on typhoon day (regular workday)

| Component | Rule | Mandatory? |
|:---|:---|:---:|
| Regular daily wage | Must pay | **Mandatory** |
| Extra pay (加給) | Should provide additional compensation | Recommended (宜) |
| Overtime (beyond 8hrs) | +1/3 first 2hrs, +2/3 next 2hrs (Art.24§1) | **Mandatory** |

**The extra pay (加給) amount is NOT specified by law.** No defined multiplier (1x, 2x, etc.).
Many companies voluntarily pay an extra 1x daily wage (double pay), but this is company policy.

### Hourly/Daily Workers (時薪制/日薪制)

#### Does NOT work on typhoon day
- **NOT entitled to pay** — no work = no pay for hourly/daily workers
- Same protections apply: cannot be treated as absence, no forced leave usage

#### DOES work on typhoon day
- Regular hourly/daily rate for hours worked — **mandatory**
- Extra pay — **recommended** (same as monthly workers)

## Pay Rules When Typhoon Falls on Special Days

### Typhoon on Rest Day (休息日)

If employer requires attendance, **mandatory** rest day overtime rates apply (勞基法第24條第2項):

| Hours | Rate |
|:---:|:---:|
| First 2 hours | Hourly wage x **1.34** (加給 1/3) |
| Hours 3–8 | Hourly wage x **1.67** (加給 2/3) |
| Hours 9+ | Hourly wage x **2.67** (加給 8/3) |

### Typhoon on Regular Day Off (例假日) or National Holiday (國定假日)

Under **勞基法第40條** (disaster/emergency provision):

| Component | Rule |
|:---|:---|
| Extra pay | **+1x daily wage** (double pay) — mandatory |
| Compensatory day off | **Must grant make-up day off** — mandatory |
| Reporting | Must report to local labor authority within **24 hours** |
| Rest | Must provide **12 hours continuous rest** afterward |

This only applies to genuine emergency/essential work during disasters.

## Calculation Formulas

### Hourly Wage Derivation (from monthly salary)

```
Hourly wage = Monthly salary / 30 / 8
```

### Typhoon Day Overtime (Regular Workday)

```
First 2 OT hours:  hourly_wage x 4/3 x 2
Next 2 OT hours:   hourly_wage x 5/3 x 2
```

### Typhoon Day on Rest Day (Full 8 Hours)

```
Rest day premium = (hourly_wage x 4/3 x 2) + (hourly_wage x 5/3 x 6)
```

### Example: Monthly salary NT$36,000, works 8 hours on typhoon rest day

```
Hourly wage = 36,000 / 30 / 8 = NT$150
Premium = (150 x 4/3 x 2) + (150 x 5/3 x 6)
        = (150 x 1.34 x 2) + (150 x 1.67 x 6)
        = 402 + 1,503
        = NT$1,905 overtime pay (on top of base salary already included)
```

### Example: Monthly salary NT$30,000, typhoon on national holiday, works 8 hours

```
Daily wage = 30,000 / 30 = NT$1,000
Extra pay = 1x daily wage = NT$1,000
Total for the day = NT$1,000 (base, already in salary) + NT$1,000 (extra) = NT$2,000
Plus: employer must grant a compensatory day off
```

## Transportation Allowance (交通津貼)

When employer requires attendance during a typhoon:

| Obligation | Detail |
|:---|:---|
| Transportation assistance | Employer SHOULD provide vehicles, allowances, or assistance |
| Taxi fare | If normal commute is unavailable and taxi is necessary, employer SHOULD pay taxi costs |
| Fixed amount | **No specified amount** — situation-dependent |
| Injury liability | Commute injury during employer-required typhoon attendance = **occupational injury (職業災害)** under 勞基法第59條 |

## Partial-Day Typhoon Scenarios

| Scenario | Hours Worked | Remaining Hours |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Closure announced mid-day (e.g., 4PM) | Regular pay for worked hours (mandatory) | Pay for remaining hours is optional |
| Closure announced before workday | Full typhoon day rules apply | -- |
| Closure lifted mid-day | Employer may request return; workers who cannot return should not be penalized | -- |

## Worker Safety Rights

| Right | Legal Basis |
|:---|:---|
| Right to refuse dangerous work (立即危險退避權) | 職業安全衛生法第18條 |
| Employer must implement safety measures | 職業安全衛生法 |
| Outdoor workers: employer must provide safety protective facilities | Fine up to NT$300,000 for violations |
| No adverse action for exercising safety rights | Cannot dismiss, transfer, or withhold wages |

---

## Summary: Employer MUST vs SHOULD

### MUST (mandatory, enforceable)
- Must NOT treat non-attendance as unauthorized absence
- Must NOT force leave usage or require make-up work
- Must NOT deduct attendance bonuses
- Must pay regular wages when requiring attendance
- Must pay statutory overtime rates (rest day / holiday)
- Must report Art.40 emergency overtime within 24 hours
- Must implement safety measures for attending workers

### SHOULD (recommended, not penalty-backed)
- Should not deduct wages when workers cannot attend (monthly salary)
- Should provide extra pay when requiring attendance on regular workday
- Should provide transportation assistance / taxi fare
