## Special Identity Details

### Foreign Workers (外籍勞工)

**Industrial foreign workers** (manufacturing, construction, agriculture, fishing, caregiving in institutions):
- **Same rates and sharing as regular employees** — no difference from domestic workers
- LI: Employee 20%, Employer 70%, Government 10%
- NHI (Cat.1-2): Employee 30%, Employer 60%, Government 10%
- OAI: 100% Employer

**Domestic foreign workers / caregivers (家事外勞/家庭看護工)**:
- **LI: NOT mandatory** (voluntary) — excluded from Labour Standards Act
- **OAI: MANDATORY since May 1, 2022** — 100% employer-funded
- **NHI: MANDATORY** — Employee 30%, Employer 60%, Government 10%
- Non-compliance OAI fines: NT$20,000–NT$100,000
- Employer must enroll in NHI within 3 days of residence permit

### Government Agency Employees (政府機關員工)

- If enrolled in LI (not Government Employee Insurance/公保): same sharing as regular employees
- NHI falls under **Category 1-1**: Employee 30%, Employer (government agency) **70%**, Government 0%

### Self-Employed (自營作業者)

- Must enroll through an occupational union (職業工會)
- LI: Self 60%, Government 40% (ordinary accident only, no employment insurance)
- NHI: If Cat.1-3 (self-employed professional): Self **100%**
- NHI: If enrolled via union as Cat.2: Self 60%, Government 40%
- OAI: Mandatory via union; worker 60%, government 40%

## Disability Premium Subsidies (身心障礙者保費補助)

Legal basis: 身心障礙者參加社會保險保險費補助辦法

Applies to: LI, NHI, Employment Insurance, National Pension, Government Employee Insurance

| Disability Level | Chinese | Subsidy on Self-Paid Portion |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Extreme/Severe (極重度/重度) | 極重度、重度 | **100%** (fully subsidized) |
| Moderate (中度) | 中度 | **50%** |
| Mild (輕度) | 輕度 | **25%** |

**Important notes:**
- Subsidy covers the **insured person's self-paid portion only** (not employer's share)
- Automatically applied after disability certification — no separate application needed
- Example: Regular employee with moderate disability, LI insured salary NT$36,300
  - Normal employee share: NT$36,300 x 12.5% x 20% = NT$908
  - After 50% subsidy: NT$908 x 50% = **NT$454** (government covers remaining NT$454)

### Calculation Formula

```
Subsidized premium = Normal self-paid premium x (1 - subsidy_rate)
Government subsidy = Normal self-paid premium x subsidy_rate
```

| Disability | subsidy_rate | Employee pays | Government covers |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Severe | 1.00 | 0% | 100% |
| Moderate | 0.50 | 50% | 50% |
| Mild | 0.25 | 75% | 25% |

## Low-Income Household Subsidies (低收入戶/中低收入戶)

### Low-Income (低收入戶)
- **NHI: 100% government-subsidized** (NHI Category 5)
- National Pension: 100% government-subsidized
- If employed: standard LI rates apply (employer still pays 70%)

### Mid-Low Income (中低收入戶)
- **NHI: 50% subsidy** on the insured person's self-paid portion
- Added under Social Assistance Act amendments, effective July 1, 2011
- If also disabled, the **higher** subsidy takes precedence

### Combined Disability + Low-Income

When both disabilities and income subsidies apply, the higher subsidy rate wins:

| Scenario | Effective Subsidy |
|:---|:---:|
| Mid-low income + mild disability (25%) | 50% (mid-low income is higher) |
| Mid-low income + moderate disability (50%) | 50% (same) |
| Mid-low income + severe disability (100%) | 100% (disability is higher) |
| Low-income + any disability | 100% (low-income NHI is 100%) |

## Indigenous Workers (原住民)

- **No separate LI/NHI premium subsidy** specifically for indigenous status
- Follow the same rates as any other worker in their category
- Indigenous Peoples' Employment Rights Protection Act focuses on employment quotas, not premium subsidies
- Unemployed indigenous persons aged 55-64: National Pension supplement of NT$3,000/month (原住民給付)
